> [!NOTE]
> The vAPI Foundation is currently being incorporated. Until incorporation completes, Echelon Finance Ltd, a corporation incorporated in the Republic of the Marshall Islands (registration no. 127117), is the legal operator and counterparty of vAPI Network.

**What the current operator does.** Echelon Finance Ltd funds development, publishes network records, and is the network's current legal counterparty. It does not control user payment keys or hold admin keys over the token.

**Does vAPI hold my funds?** No. vAPI never holds buyer funds and never holds the keys that move them. Every payment is signed on the buyer's machine. The buyer-local wallet keeps its encrypted payment key on the buyer's machine and signs one exact authorization per call. Keep only a small working balance in it and sweep unused funds back.

**Is token governance active?** No. Governance controls are not active. See [Roadmap & status](/products/roadmap-status/).

**What is the audit status?** Hacken completed a security audit of the vAPI token and its Uniswap V4 fee hook. [Read the report on hacken.io](https://hacken.io/audits/vapi-network/sca-vapi-network-sc-audit-jul2026/) or see [Audits](/protocol/audits/) for what the report covers.
