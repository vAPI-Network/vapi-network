The vAPI Genesis Goose Eggs are the network's founding NFT campaign. Each Egg is a numbered digital collectible on Base, and the collection is capped at 1,000 Eggs.

> [!TIP]
> The public mint is open at 9 USDC or the equivalent in ETH, one Egg per wallet. [Mint an Egg](https://api.vapinetwork.ai/eggs/).

Every Egg shares the same artwork and carries its own edition number on the shell.

## How the campaign went

1. Application. Applying was free, one application per person and per wallet. Applicants qualified with X actions or a verified email.
2. Applicant mint. After applications closed, each eligible applicant wallet could mint one Egg for 3 USDC on Base during a fourteen-day window. The window closed on 30 September 2026 around 13:00 UTC, and unused applicant mint rights expired.
3. Public mint. Since 30 September 2026, any wallet can mint one of the remaining Eggs for 9 USDC, or for the ETH amount quoted when the mint is authorized. The public mint runs until all 1,000 Eggs are minted or vAPI Foundation ends it.

## Mint an Egg

Open the [mint page](https://api.vapinetwork.ai/eggs/), sign in with your wallet on Base, and pass the bot check. No application is needed. Accept the Official Rules, then choose USDC or ETH.

| Pay with | Transactions                            | Note                                                   |
| -------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| USDC     | Two: approve exactly 9 USDC, then mint  | Keep a little ETH for gas                              |
| ETH      | One: mint, paying the quoted ETH amount | The quote holds for 15 minutes; keep extra ETH for gas |

The contract is new, so your wallet may label it untrusted. Before you sign, check that the contract address in your wallet matches the one below.
