The [Profile](/products/earn/profile/) badge wall groups badges under "vAPI badge" and "Quest badge".
It shows group counts, locked and earned states, and "New" awards.
"Closest to earning" points to a nearby milestone.
Click a badge to open its detail.
A badge stays earned even if the underlying number later drops.
Badges add no points and do not change rank or campaign rewards.

| Badge                    | How you earn it                                                                                 |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Picture set              | Upload a profile picture.                                                                       |
| Fully linked             | Link X, Discord, Telegram and GitHub.                                                           |
| First check-in           | Complete your first daily check-in.                                                             |
| 3 day streak             | Reach a check-in streak of three days.                                                          |
| 7 day streak             | Reach a check-in streak of seven days.                                                          |
| 30 day streak            | Reach a check-in streak of 30 days.                                                             |
| First friend             | Have one invited friend join.                                                                   |
| 5 friends                | Have five invited friends join.                                                                 |
| 25 friends               | Have 25 invited friends join.                                                                   |
| 10 quests                | Complete 10 distinct quests on the current quest board.                                         |
| 25 quests                | Complete 25 distinct quests on the current quest board.                                         |
| Social section cleared   | Complete every quest in a Social section that has at least one quest.                           |
| Platform section cleared | Complete every quest in a Platform section that has at least one quest.                         |
| 10 paid calls            | Make 10 paid Calls within 60 days across your wallet and linked-agent wallets.                  |
| 50 paid calls            | Make 50 paid Calls within 60 days across your wallet and linked-agent wallets.                  |
| First payout             | Receive a USDC payment for your listing from a buyer outside your own and linked-agent wallets. |
| 10k staker               | Have at least 10,000 active staked vAPI.                                                        |
| Agent funded             | Have a linked agent wallet pay for a Router top-up on your account.                             |
| 10 agents                | Have 10 agents linked at the same time.                                                         |
| Active agent             | Have a linked agent make a paid Call or Router request.                                         |

Some quests award a badge.
Those awards appear under "Quest badge".

Older completed quests with an attached badge also show that badge as earned at the completion time.
This retrospective display works even without a stored engine badge award.
It creates no new-award Activity entry.
