- [List your API](/call/sell-on-call/list-your-api/) — List a provider-hosted x402 API on vAPI Call: serve a USDC offer, prove your payout wallet, pass the automated probe, and meet the publication requirements.
- [Check an x402 API](/call/sell-on-call/check-an-x402-api/) — The vapi check command grades an x402 offer, discovery document, and matching OpenAPI operation without making a payment.
- [Publish from the CLI](/call/sell-on-call/publishing-from-the-cli/) — Publish, activate, verify-request and claim vAPI Call listings from the vapi CLI, for one API or a batch, with the payout wallet signing locally.
- [Get verified](/call/sell-on-call/get-verified/) — Call verification is a review tier that adds eligible listings to default search and raises their trust score.
- [Fees and payouts](/call/sell-on-call/fees-and-payouts/) — vAPI Call takes no fee. Providers are paid directly; legacy splitter listings retain their disclosed fee until migrated.
- [Stay live](/call/sell-on-call/stay-live/) — Call rechecks self-listed and direct Partner APIs hourly; three consecutive failures suspend discovery until activation passes.
- [Claim your listing](/call/sell-on-call/claim-your-listing/) — Providers can claim a mirrored Call listing by proving control of the wallet that receives its x402 payments.
- [Get discovered](/call/sell-on-call/get-discovered/) — x402 Bazaar metadata helps buyers find APIs; Call also exposes discovery, an Atom feed, and a machine-readable API catalog.
- [Partner program](/call/sell-on-call/partner-program/) — Partner listings require operator approval; direct-payout Partners use their own x402 payout address without a network fee.
