You fund the work and define what counts as completion. Choose a task for one worker, a bounty for selected submissions or a pool for repeated claims. Check each shape's cancellation and payment rules before funding.

## Write the terms

Describe the result, acceptance criteria and proof you need. For a task, divide the budget into 1 to 5 milestones with amounts and deadlines. Add check-ins if progress reports help you catch blocked work before a deadline.

## Fund and choose a worker

Check the terms hash and platform fee before funding the full USDC amount. A task can hold funds before you choose a worker. Accept a proposal or invite a worker, then record the award with their signed consent to the scope.

Hiring from a published offer lets you choose the worker in advance. Funding still leaves the contract Open until the award records that worker's consent.

Agree any worker bond in the terms; this version supports bonds only on one-milestone tasks. The worker funds it separately at award.

## Review a milestone

Compare the delivered proof with the acceptance criteria. Approve it to release that milestone's worker allocation. If it falls short, reject within the review window and give a specific reason.

Rejection returns the milestone to pending; it does not extend the original delivery deadline. A resubmission starts a new review window. If you leave a submitted milestone unrejected and undisputed, anyone can finalize payment after its review window ends.

Use [Reviews and disputes](/tasks/reviews-and-disputes/) promptly when you need a contested outcome. You can dispute a fresh delivery until the review deadline or 2 days after delivery, whichever comes first; you can reject it until the review deadline instead. A rejected milestone can be disputed after two rejections. It can also be disputed after one rejection once the deadline has passed or the remainder has closed. Either way, the dispute must come within 2 days of that rejection. If a tie restores the milestone, these windows start again from the restore, and only you can raise a new dispute on a restored fresh delivery.

## Handle stalled work

Contact the worker when a check-in is missing or reports a blocker. A Stalled label alone does not let you withdraw funds. You can cancel all pending milestones once a pending milestone's deadline plus cancellation grace has passed, but only if no rejected pending milestone is still inside its 2-day dispute window after that rejection.

Cancellation grace defaults to 1 day. Submitted and disputed milestones keep their funds.

## Choose bounty winners

Wait until the submission deadline, then review the unsealed proof. Choose eligible submitters with active commitments and award within the choice window. The contract pays each selected winner the fixed reward and refunds unused rewards in the same award.

A funded bounty cannot be cancelled early, even if you see no useful submissions. If you choose nobody, anyone can finalize a full refund after the choice window. Submitters have no onchain milestone dispute route against that choice in this version.

## Manage a pool

Choose yourself as verifier or explicitly delegate verification to another address. A vAPI checker can approve claims only when you choose it as verifier. Verification authorizes payouts from your pool budget, test USDC during the beta.

Pause payouts when you need to review claims or change the verifier. You can close a pool early and recover the unused budget. Closing prevents unpaid claims from receiving money, so state that risk in your claim terms.
